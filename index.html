💡 How to Scan & View Your AR Video:

  1. Standard phone camera apps WILL NOT WORK directly on the PNG file (AR markers are custom patterns, not standard QR codes).
  2. Display or Print the PNG Marker: Open/download the printable-marker-frame.png and display it on your laptop screen, another phone screen, or print it out.
  3. Open the WebAR Scanner: Open this WebAR app on your phone browser or PC, go to 📷 AR Scanner tab, and click 🚀 Start Scanner.
  4. Point Camera at the PNG Picture: Point your scanner camera directly at the displayed/printed marker image. Your video will pop up and play over the picture!
